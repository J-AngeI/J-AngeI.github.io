How to play

Build a circuit whose output matches the truth table on the right. Switches on the left can be clicked to turn them on and off, and lights on the right turn on when they receive power. In the truth table, "on" means true and "off" means false. Neither switches nor lights can be moved.

  • Place a gate: click a gate button at the bottom of the screen, then click again to set it down.
  • Connect things: press C to toggle connection mode. In this mode you can link switches, gates, and lights to one another, or click a wire to delete it.
  • Delete a gate: click the gate, then click the square "trashcan" in the bottom-left.

A switch can power any number of gates, but each gate input or light can only receive power from one source at a time.

Toggle the switches to test your circuit. Each row of the truth table turns green when your circuit gets it right. Solve every row to advance to the next level.
